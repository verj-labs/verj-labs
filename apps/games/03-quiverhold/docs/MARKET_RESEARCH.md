# Quiverhold: market research

Research done on 2026-11-28 to choose the genre for game 3 on numbers rather than feel. It covers
three questions in the order we asked them: is the first idea (Satisfactory meets Command &
Conquer, as a tower defense) any good; which genre has the best odds; and how the final idea fits.

**Reading the numbers.** Valve publishes no sales data, so almost every figure here is a
third-party estimate. A "hit" means 1,000+ Steam reviews; a common rule of thumb puts that at
about US$150,000 gross. Sources disagree in places, and that is noted where it matters.

---

## 1. The first idea: factory automation plus tower defense

**Verdict:** proven demand, heavy competition. "Factory + tower defense" is a shelf on Steam, not a
hook.

- **Automation is a strong genre, filling fast.** Satisfactory leads automation games by players
  on Steam ([wasdland](https://www.wasdland.com/games/automation/most-played)); Factorio passed
  90,000 players at once with Space Age
  ([ReadWrite](https://readwrite.com/factorios-space-age-dlc-helps-the-daddy-of-factory-games-smash-through-its-steam-record-bringing-players-flooding-back/)).
  Releases: 450 in 2025, about 822 by late 2026
  ([Steam Page Analyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/niches/automation)).
- **Factory + tower defense already exists:** Mindustry (8M+ Android installs, Overwhelmingly
  Positive on Steam, but free or cheap: [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/io.anuke.mindustry)),
  Tower Factory, ShapeHero Factory, Riftbreaker, They Are Billions, AUTOMATE DEATH.
- **Base-building defence has the indie hits:** Thronefall 1M+ copies
  ([Games Market](https://www.gamesmarket.global/indie-hit-thronefall-sells-over-one-million-copies-588b03986d04328e71544cbcb1d1b454/)),
  Cataclismo 250k+ ([WN Hub](https://wnhub.io/news/finance/item-47519)).
- **Command & Conquer-style RTS is weak commercially:** 2025 was a good year for RTS reviews but had
  no breakout, and Stormgate launched poorly
  ([GamePressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/newsroom/2025-is-the-strongest-year-for-rts-in-ages-but-the-big-breakout-h/zc8400)).
  Unit micro and multiplayer are also too big for one developer.

## 2. Which genre: the numbers

Steam niche index, September 2026 ([Steam Page Analyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/niches)).
Hit rate = share of games released 2024-01-01 to 2025-06-30 that reached 1,000 reviews; the Steam
average is **4.2%**. Supply growth = 2026 releases against the same weeks of 2025 (Steam overall
+45%). Median gross for paid games from [Immutable](https://www.immutable.com/insights/steam-genre-revenue-benchmarks).

| Niche | Hit rate | Supply growth 2026 | Median gross | Solo-feasible |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Open-world survival craft | **28.3%** (6.7×) | +39% | $102,000 | no (3D, large scope) |
| Colony sim | **13.7%** (3.3×) | +53% | $17,300 | yes |
| Shop keeper | 13.2% (3.1×) | **+634%** | — | yes, but flooding |
| Bullet heaven | 13.1% (3.1×) | **+406%** | — | yes, but flooding |
| Cozy | 11% (2.6×) | +143% | — | yes, but flooding |
| City builder | 10.5% (2.5×) | +27% | — | yes |
| Souls-like | 8.9% | +18% | $8,460 | no |
| Automation | 8.4% | +170% | — | yes, but flooding |
| Farming sim | 8% | +37% | $8,690 | yes |
| Roguelike deckbuilder | 7.8% | +93% | — | yes |
| Psychological horror | 5.8% | +28% | $2,590 | yes |
| Idle / incremental | 5.6% | +150% | $2,870 | yes, cheap ($3–15) |
| **Tower defense** | **3.5% (below average)** | +62% | — | yes |
| RTS | — | — | $16,700 | partly |

What the table says:

1. **Tower defense on its own is below the Steam average**, and it swings year to year
   ([HowToMarketAGame](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/05/14/2026-q1-games/)). Defence belongs in
   the game as a feature, not as the genre it is sold under.
2. **Avoid the gold rushes:** shop keeper, bullet heaven, cozy, automation and idle have good hit
   rates measured on 2024–25 games, but supply is up 140–630% with thousands of unreleased pages
   queued.
3. **Survival craft's high rate is partly about who attempts it:** about 90 releases a year, mostly
   funded teams. A second source agrees on the rate (20.8% in 2025,
   [HowToMarketAGame](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/01/27/what-the-hell-happened-in-2025/)).
4. **Sources disagree on city builders:** 10.5% in one, 0.5% (2 of 397) in the other. We do not
   lean on that row.
5. **Social co-op ("friendslop") sells the most copies but is a lottery:** R.E.P.O. 18.5M, PEAK
   15.4M ([AppMagic via Game Dev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/appmagic-friendslop-games-in-2025)),
   solo-made Meccha Chameleon 3M+ in a week
   ([GamerMarkt](https://www.gamermarkt.com/blog/meccha-chameleon-solo-dev-two-months-3-million-steam-sales/)),
   but no hit-rate data and about 3% 30-day retention.
6. **The overall market is harsh:** the median paid game released on Steam in 2026 has grossed
   about $1,700 ([Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/2026/09/01/the-median-revenue-of-games-on-steam-has-been-declining-for-12-consecutive-years)).
7. **PC over mobile for this kind of game:** mobile revenue was flat in 2025 while PC grew 13%
   ([GameDev.net](https://gamedev.net/news/1834-mobile-revenue-remained-flat-across-2025-but-pc-gaming-sees-another-record-year/));
   the only growing mobile segment, hybrid-casual (+20%), needs a paid acquisition budget
   ([Sensor Tower via Game Dev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-2026)).

**Recommendation:** a colony sim / base builder with defence, on Steam. Colony sims also sell for
years: Timberborn about $24M and Going Medieval about $9.4M
([games-stats](https://games-stats.com/blog/Steam_in_Q12026/)).

## 3. The final idea and how it fits

The idea that came out of the discussion: a survival settlement on an open map, build anywhere
(Command & Conquer / Red Alert style), expand toward resources, and turn resources into
ammunition (wood → arrows → archer towers). Inspired by Surviving the Aftermath, with the focus on
defence instead of city survival.

- **Where it sits:** Colony Sim / City Builder / Base Building / Survival, the stronger buckets, with
  Tower Defense only as a secondary tag.
- **Closest games:** They Are Billions, Diplomacy is Not an Option, Age of Darkness: Final Stand
  (1.0 in January 2025, [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/press-release/age-of-darkness-final-stand-emerges-from-early-access-with-full-release-multiplayer)),
  Cataclismo, Thronefall, Surviving the Aftermath.
- **What none of the big ones do:** make ammunition a real supply chain. That is our hook.
- **Direct competitors on the hook (small, mostly unreleased):**
  - **Resupply:** tower defense where workers, belts and bots carry ammo to turrets; planned Q1 2027
    ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5504090), [IndieDB](https://www.indiedb.com/games/resupply)).
  - **Infinitory:** towers need specific ammunition ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3222280)).
  - **Feed the Front:** a sci-fi RTS where resources and ammo fly between buildings.
  - **Terrors of War:** a WW2 base-building RTS where depots feed defences with ammo
    ([playtester.io](https://playtester.io/terrors-of-war)).
  The space is open but not empty; ship a demo reasonably soon.

## 4. Complexity

To build it is mid-to-high complexity, about 3–5× Flintkeep's work (an estimate). The hard systems
are horde pathfinding, goods logistics and the worker job system, and they are also the hook. The
playable complexity is our choice; we aim for medium (learnable in five minutes, deep in
decisions). Estimated solo timeline: prototype 2–3 weeks, vertical slice about 3 months, Early
Access 9–15 months. Details in [`GAME_DESIGN.md`](GAME_DESIGN.md#13-milestones).
